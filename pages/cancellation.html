Cancellation & Refund Policy

At Desert Bloom Resort & Camp, we understand that plans can change. We strive to be as flexible as possible while ensuring we can maintain the high quality of service our guests expect.

1. Cancellation Policy

  • 7+ Days Prior: Cancellations made 7 days or more before the check-in date are eligible for a full refund (minus any applicable transaction processing fees).
  • 3-6 Days Prior: Cancellations made between 3 and 6 days before check-in will incur a 50% cancellation charge.
  • Within 48 Hours: Cancellations made within 48 hours of the check-in date, or "No Shows," are non-refundable.

2. Refund Process

If your cancellation is approved for a refund:

  • The refund will be processed to the original payment method used during booking.
  • Please allow 7–10 business days for the amount to reflect in your account.
  • Refunds are subject to bank/payment gateway processing times.

3. Booking Modifications

If you need to change your dates rather than cancel, please contact us directly. We will do our best to accommodate your new dates based on availability. Changes must be requested at least 72 hours before the original check-in date.
