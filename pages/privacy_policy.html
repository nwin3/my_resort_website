Privacy Policy

At Desert Bloom Resort & Camp, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or book a stay with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a reservation or contact us, we may collect personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, and travel preferences. This helps us ensure you have a comfortable and personalized experience at our resort.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Process and confirm your booking requests.
  • Communicate updates regarding your stay.
  • Improve our website performance and guest services.
  • Send occasional updates or special offers (only if you have opted in).

3. Data Protection

We take robust security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
