Pet Policy & Guidelines

At Desert Bloom Camp & Resort, we welcome well-mannered companion pets. Because our property is situated in an open desert ecosystem with shared common camps, dunes, and wildlife, adherence to this policy ensures the safety of all guests, staff, and your pets.

1. Pet Charges & Room Allocation

  • Daily Pet Fee: A charge of ₹1,500/- per day applies for bringing a pet. This fee covers specialized post-departure sanitization and maintenance.
  • Pet Limit: A maximum of 1 pet per room/tent is permitted across all accommodation types.
  • Advance Notification: Guests traveling with a pet must inform management at the time of booking to ensure placement in an allocated pet-friendly room.

2. Eligibility & Health Certification

  • Permitted Pets: Domestic dogs and cats only.
  • Vaccination & Deworming: Pets must be up-to-date with Rabies and core vaccinations, as well as current tick and flea prevention. A valid vaccination card or vet certificate must be presented at check-in.

3. Leash & Supervision Standards

  • Restraint on Camp Grounds: Pets must remain on a secure handheld leash (maximum 6 feet) or inside a carrier at all times when outside your private tent or room.
  • Unattended Pets: Pets must not be left unattended in tents or rooms unless safely secured inside a well-ventilated travel crate.
  • Housekeeping Protocol: For staff safety, room servicing and maintenance will only occur when the owner is present or the pet is safely crated.

4. Restricted Zones & Cleanliness

  • Restricted Areas: To comply with hygiene standards, pets are not permitted inside dining halls, live buffet areas, kitchen zones, or cultural program performance stages.
  • Waste Cleanup: Owners are strictly responsible for cleaning up after their pet immediately. Waste must be bagged securely and deposited in designated outdoor bins.
  • Furniture & Linens: Pets are not allowed on resort beds, couches, or linens. Please bring your pet's own bedding and essentials.

5. Liability, Disturbances & Damages

  • Guest Liability: The pet owner assumes 100% financial and legal responsibility for any personal injury, property damage, or deep-cleaning requirements caused by their animal.
  • Damage Deposit: A standard refundable deposit may be collected at check-in. Any damage to tent fabric, furnishings, or linens will be billed at actual repair or replacement cost.
  • Disturbances: Continuous barking, aggressive behavior, or distress calls will not be tolerated. The resort reserves the right to ask the guest to make alternative off-site boarding arrangements if peace and safety are compromised.

6. Bookings via Third-Party Platforms (OTAs)

If you booked through an external portal (such as Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Agoda, or Goibibo), please verify that your selected rate plan explicitly allows pet boarding. Third-party room reservations do not waive the required daily pet fee of ₹1,500/-, pet security deposits, health verifications, or on-site behavioural rules.
