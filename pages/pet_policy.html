Pet Policy & Guidelines

At Desert Bloom Camp & Resort, we welcome well-mannered companion pets. Because our property is situated in an open desert ecosystem with shared common camps, dunes, and wildlife, adherence to this policy ensures the safety of all guests, staff, and your pets.

1. Prior Approval & Pet Eligibility

  • Advance Notification: Guests traveling with pets must inform management prior to arrival to verify availability in pet-friendly accommodation units.
  • Permitted Pets: Domestic dogs and cats only. A maximum limit of two pets per tent/room applies.
  • Vaccination & Health: Pets must be current on Rabies and core vaccinations. Proof of vaccination records or a vet certificate must be available upon check-in.

2. Leash & Supervision Standards

  • Restraint on Camp Grounds: Pets must be kept on a handheld leash (maximum 6 feet) or secured in an appropriate carrier at all times when outside your tent or cottage.
  • Unattended Pets: Pets must not be left unattended in tents or rooms unless comfortably secured inside a breathable travel crate.
  • Housekeeping Protocol: For staff safety, room servicing and maintenance will only occur when the owner is present or the pet is safely crated.

3. Restricted Zones & Cleanliness

  • Restricted Areas: To comply with local hygiene standards, pets are not allowed inside the indoor dining hall, food buffet counters, kitchen areas, or cultural program performance stages.
  • Waste Cleanup: Owners are strictly responsible for bagging and properly disposing of all pet waste in designated outdoor bins immediately.
  • Furniture & Bedding: Pets are not permitted on guest beds, couches, or linens. Please bring your companion's personal bedding or travel blanket.

4. Liability, Noise & Damages

  • Guest Liability: The pet owner assumes 100% financial and legal responsibility for any personal injury, property damage, or deep-cleaning requirements caused by the animal.
  • Damage Deposit & Surcharges: A standard refundable deposit may be collected at check-in. Any claw tears to tent fabric, chewed furnishings, or soiled mattresses will be charged at actual replacement costs.
  • Noise & Behavioral Disturbances: To preserve the tranquility of the desert, persistent barking, aggression, or distress calls will not be permitted. The resort reserves the right to ask owners to arrange alternative off-site boarding if disruptive behavior continues.

5. Bookings via Third-Party Platforms (OTAs)

If you booked through an external portal (such as Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Agoda, or Goibibo), please verify that your selected rate plan explicitly allows pet boarding. Third-party room reservations do not waive the required pet security deposits, health verifications, or on-site behavioral rules.
