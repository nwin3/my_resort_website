Discover Jaisalmer

Jaisalmer Fort

The iconic "Golden Fort," one of the few living forts in the world, filled with narrow alleys, temples, and shops.

Sam Sand Dunes

The heart of desert adventure. Perfect for camel safaris, jeep rides, and spectacular sunset photography.

Gadisar Lake

An artificial reservoir built in 1367. Ideal for peaceful boating and bird watching near ancient temples.

Patwon Ki Haveli

A cluster of five elaborate havelis known for intricate carvings, mirror work, and stunning architecture.

Bada Bagh

A garden complex featuring royal cenotaphs of the Maharajas, offering some of the best sunset vistas.

Kuldhara Village

Known as the "Haunted Village," this abandoned settlement offers a mysterious and historical glimpse into the past.

Desert National Park

One of the largest wildlife parks in India, home to the Great Indian Bustard and unique desert flora/fauna.

Jain Temples

Located within the fort, these 12th-century marvels are world-famous for their incredible sandstone filigree work.

Nathmal Ji Ki Haveli

Famous for its unique blend of Rajput and Islamic architecture, showcasing the mastery of 19th-century craftsmen.

Khaba Fort

A ruined fortress overlooking a deserted village, perfect for those seeking off-the-beaten-path experiences.
