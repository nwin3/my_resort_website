Frequently Asked Questions About Our Jaisalmer Desert Camp

Everything you need to know about booking your stay, safaris, and family packages at Sam Sand Dunes.

How far is Desert Bloom Camp & Resort from Sam Sand Dunes and Jaisalmer city?
Desert Bloom Camp & Resort is located right beside Sam Sand Dunes, approximately 40–42 km (a 45-minute scenic drive) west of Jaisalmer city, Jaisalmer Railway Station, and Jaisalmer Airport. Our prime location gives guests immediate access to camel safaris, 4x4 jeep dune bashing, and sunset viewpoints without long transfers.
What is included in an overnight Jaisalmer desert camp package?
Our overnight desert camp packages include a traditional Rajasthani welcome drink, accommodation in an AC Royal Swiss Tent with a private en-suite bathroom, sunset camel or jeep safari on Sam Sand Dunes, evening tea with snacks, live Manganiyar folk music and Kalbeliya dance around a bonfire, a traditional Rajasthani buffet dinner, and breakfast the following morning.
Is Desert Bloom Camp safe and suitable for families with children and senior citizens?
Yes. We are a family-friendly desert resort featuring a secure, gated property with 24/7 staff assistance and full power backup. Our spacious Family Desert Suites include modern attached western bathrooms with hot and cold water, climate control, and comfortable bedding tailored for children and senior travelers.
What is the best time to visit Sam Sand Dunes in Jaisalmer?
The peak and most pleasant season to visit Jaisalmer desert camps is from October to March, when daytime temperatures are mild and nights are crisp—ideal for bonfires, stargazing, and outdoor dune dining. During warmer months, our air-conditioned luxury tents ensure complete indoor comfort.
Do we need to pre-book private dune dining, telescope stargazing, or quad biking?
Yes, exclusive experiences like Private Candlelight Dinner on the Dunes and Guided Telescope Stargazing require prior confirmation so our team can arrange private setups and equipment. Adventure activities such as ATV quad biking, paramotoring, and extended 4x4 jeep safaris can be added during booking or arranged at the camp desk.
